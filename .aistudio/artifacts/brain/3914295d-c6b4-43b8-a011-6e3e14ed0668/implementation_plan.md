# Obsidian Lattice — Interactive 3D AI/ML & Blockchain Engineer Portfolio

An interactive, scroll-choreographed 3D WebGL portfolio engineered for an AI, Machine Learning, and Blockchain specialist. Built in a restrained monochrome palette of deep black, crisp white, and architectural greys—accented only by razor-thin cyan micro-details—it guides visitors from a clean opening 3D neural network framed by splitting modular blockchain blocks down through floating crystalline monoliths, a concentric ring portal, and a morphing point-cloud social pedestal.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Delivers a continuous, single-canvas 3D spatial narrative driven by scroll, pointer parallax, and click inspection across four interconnected chapters:
  1. **Summit — Clean Neural Network & Modular Blockchain Shell**: Opens cleanly on a sculpted 3D Neural Network (multi-layer tensor nodes connected by fine hairline edges with micro-pulses of cyan data) nestled within modular beveled obsidian blocks that separate smoothly along vector lines as you interact or begin scrolling.
  2. **The Drift — Floating Project Monoliths**: Four faceted black-glass monoliths suspended in dark monochrome mist, each enclosing a distinct rotating 3D engineering sculpture (Transformer Attention Lattice, Zero-Knowledge Proof Circuit, Decentralized Inference Mesh, Autonomous On-Chain Agent Graph).
  3. **The Iris — Concentric Cryptographic Ring Portal**: A vertical camera dive through counter-rotating matte black and brushed white-silver machined rings framing an illuminated center aperture.
  4. **The Pedestal — Morphing Point-Cloud Showcase**: A sleek circular pedestal at the base of the world featuring a 12,000-particle monochrome sculpture that smoothly morphs between geometric emblems for each contact/research channel (GitHub, X, Hugging Face, LinkedIn, Email) before looping back to the summit.
- **Target Audience**: AI research labs, Web3 protocol founders, engineering leaders, and technical recruiters evaluating systems-level depth.
- **Key Value**: Lets the engineering medium speak for itself—combining minimal, editorial black-and-white poise with real GLSL particle physics, procedural 3D architecture, and interactive case studies.

### Visual Identity & Theme

- **Aesthetic Direction**: Ultra-clean, sexy architectural minimalism—monochrome obsidian black, matte charcoal greys, and crisp white with surgical restraint. Every line, margin, and 3D bevel feels intentional and quiet, avoiding visual noise.
- **Color Palette & Mood (Strict Monochrome + Micro-Cyan)**:
  - *Primary Canvas*: Deep obsidian black (`#050608`) with matching dark fog and sculpted matte charcoal terrain (`#0E1014`).
  - *Structural Surfaces*: Smoked charcoal overlays (`rgba(10, 12, 15, 0.88)`), slate grey midtones (`#3F3F46` / `#71717A`), crisp 1px white/10% hairline borders, and pure white (`#FAFAFA`) typography.
  - *Micro-Accent (<= 3% visual weight)*: Crisp cyan (`#22D3EE`) reserved exclusively for tiny high-precision details—synaptic pulse beads, active targeting reticle corners, active chapter marker, and subtle edge highlights.
- **Typography & Hierarchy (2+1 Font Rule)**:
  - *Display & Brand Wordmark*: `Outfit` (Medium 500 / SemiBold 600, tight tracking `-0.025em`) for sleek, architectural presence.
  - *Body & Case Study Prose*: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Regular 400 / Medium 500, `15px–16px`, `1.6` line-height) for crisp editorial readability.
  - *Indices, Coordinates & Benchmarks*: `JetBrains Mono` with `tabular-nums` for precision numerical alignment.
- **Anti-Slop & Viewport Restraint**:
  - Central 3D viewport stays 100% open and unobstructed.
  - Sparse perimeter HUD: Wordmark top-left, concise two-line manifesto top-right (Summit only), Sound toggle bottom-left, subtle chapter rail on the edge.
  - Zero decorative pulsing dots, zero `//` code-comment headers, zero pill badge boxes (unboxed metadata separated by `·` or `/`), and zero CSS `backdrop-filter` over the WebGL canvas.

### Interactive Feedback & Motion

- **Smooth Damping**: Camera spline tracking, pointer parallax, and modular block splitting use exponential damping (`THREE.MathUtils.damp`) so motion feels weighty and fluid.
- **Case Study Lightbox**: Crisp monochrome overlay (`<= 200ms` transitions) with `Escape` close, previous/next project navigation, clean system architecture block diagrams, and tabular quantitative metrics.
- **Audio Feedback**: Optional synthesized ambient hum modulated by scroll depth, paired with delicate crystalline chimes on hover/click and subtle sweeps during particle morphs.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Layered Clean Neural Network + Splitting Modular Blockchain Shell**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Build the opening hero as a clean 3D multi-layer Neural Network (distinct input, hidden, and output node clusters linked by crisp hairline edges and tiny moving cyan signal packets) framed by beveled obsidian consensus blocks that part smoothly on hover/scroll.
  - *Why*: Directly anchors the portfolio in AI/ML first glance—clean, geometric, and unmistakable—while seamlessly uniting it with modular blockchain geometry in a single cohesive 3D centerpiece.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: An opaque closed tower that hides the neural core until scrolled, which delays the AI/ML visual identity on initial load.

- **Procedural 3D Geometry & GPU Particle Morphing**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Generate all 3D models, terrain ridges, neural connections, monolith artifacts, and 5 target particle buffers (12,000 points each) procedurally in code, using custom GLSL `ShaderMaterial` for particle morphing and neural signal flow.
  - *Why*: Zero external asset 404s, instant load times, razor-sharp crispness at any resolution, and smooth 60fps GPU interpolation without per-frame memory allocation.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Static `.glb` models or CPU-bound particle loops that cause stuttering on standard laptops.

- **Restrained Monochrome Lighting & Micro-Cyan Rationing**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Use a cool neutral studio `RoomEnvironment` reflection map, crisp white key light, soft slate rim lighting, and restrict cyan strictly to tiny emissive signal dots, thin 1px reticles, and subtle specular accents.
  - *Why*: Prevents generic neon "cyberpunk" clutter and achieves the sleek, high-fashion black-white-grey editorial aesthetic requested.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Heavy bloom passes and saturated cyan glow across entire meshes, which washes out surface form and feels dated.

- **Software WebGL Guard & Reduced-Motion Fallbacks**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Detect software rasterizers (SwiftShader/llvmpipe) to adjust pixel ratio, shadows, and render pacing cleanly while keeping identical scene geometry and UI, alongside `webglcontextlost` recovery and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
  - *Why*: Guarantees smooth responsiveness across all hardware and headless verification environments.

### Data Model & State

- **Portfolio Projects Catalog**: Typed array of 4 AI/ML & Blockchain case studies (`id`, `indexLabel`, `title`, `domain`, `year`, `summary`, `architectureNodes`, `metrics` with `tabular-nums`, `stack`, `artifactType`, and `links`).
- **Social / Particle Channel Catalog**: Typed array of 5 pedestal channels (`GitHub`, `X / Twitter`, `Hugging Face`, `LinkedIn`, `Email`) paired with 3D surface-sampled particle coordinate buffers and actions.
- **Scene Runtime State (Ref-Backed)**: Continuous `scrollTarget`, `scrollCurrent`, `pointerNDC`, `towerSplitFactor`, and `morphProgress` live in mutable refs inside the animation loop so 60fps updates never cause React re-renders; React state updates only on discrete events (active chapter, hovered monolith, open modal, selected pedestal channel, sound state).

### Interactive Component & State Mapping

- **Scroll & Chapter Navigation**: Wheel/touch gestures and edge chapter dots drive `scrollTarget` (`0..1`) along a continuous 3D camera spline (`CatmullRomCurve3`), smoothly transitioning from the Summit Neural Network down past the 4 monoliths, through the concentric rings, to the particle pedestal (with seamless return-to-summit loop).
- **Opening Neural Network & Block Splitting**: Cursor proximity and initial scroll smoothly separate the modular obsidian blockchain plates outward along thin silver wireframe vectors while tiny cyan neural impulses travel across the central synaptic network.
- **Monolith Inspection**: Raycasting against simplified monolith hitboxes projects 3D coordinates to 2D screen space for clean white/cyan corner brackets; clicking pauses scroll drift, triggers a chime, and opens the full Case Study Overlay.
- **Pedestal Particle Cycling**: Left/Right HUD arrows and channel labels trigger a 1.1s GLSL curl-noise morph between pre-computed 3D particle emblems accompanied by a subtle harmonic Web Audio sweep.
